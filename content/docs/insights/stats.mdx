---
title: Statistics
description: Read app analytics from the embedded Looker Studio report.
---

**Statistics** shows a read-only analytics report (Looker Studio) for field-app usage. There is nothing to configure — interact with the embedded report directly: change date ranges, filter and drill into charts.
