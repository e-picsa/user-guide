**Resources → Admin** (requires `resources.admin`) compares the dashboard's live resource records — files, links and collections — against the resource bundle shipped inside the field app, so you can see what farmers actually have versus what has changed since.

## Sync statuses

The chips at the top summarise every resource at a glance — click one to filter the table to that status, click again to clear:

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| In Sync | Dashboard and bundled app agree. Nothing to do. |
| Differs | The record exists in both places but something changed — title, description, cover, URL, storage file, language, type or collection membership. Edit the dashboard record or rebuild the bundle to resolve it. |
| Missing in App | A dashboard record that is not in the app bundle yet — it will reach farmers in the next release. |
| Orphaned in App | A bundled copy with no matching dashboard record — it was removed from the dashboard but is still shipped in the app. |

The **Total Resources** chip clears any filter.

## Understanding the table

Each row is one file, link or collection: its **Type**, its title (**Resource / Collection**) and its **Sync Status**. Click a row to open a read-only preview showing what the farmer sees — videos play inline, PDFs render in place, links show their destination with an **Open Website** button, and collections list their member files and links. Rows marked *Bundled copy only* have no live database record behind them.

<Callout type="info">
  This page answers *is the app up to date?* — not *what should change?*.
  Make the change in Files, Links or Collections first, then cut a new app
  release to move rows back to In Sync.
</Callout>

  <AccessRow roles="admin">Filter by sync status and preview any row to see what the field app shows. The page is read-only — fixes happen by editing the underlying file, link or collection (`resources.admin`, required to access this page).</AccessRow>
