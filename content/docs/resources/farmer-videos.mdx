**Resources → Farmer Videos** is a read-only audit of the PICSA training videos: which languages each video is translated into, and the actual video files per locale.

## Translation matrix

Each cell shows the state for one video in one language: dubbed audio, subtitled, not applicable (for example testimonial videos only exist for some countries) or missing. Click a cell to preview the video.

## Video files catalog

The second tab lists the 11 videos as expandable panels. Each panel breaks down the available files by locale, country, resolution and size, with actions to **preview**, **copy the URL** or **open the original**.

<Callout type="tip">
  Use the **Total Available per Language** footer row to spot the least
  translated language at a glance.
</Callout>
