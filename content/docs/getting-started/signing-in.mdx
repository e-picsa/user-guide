---
title: Signing in
description: How to access the E-PICSA Dashboard and what you see on arrival.
---

The E-PICSA Dashboard uses email and password sign-in. Your account is created by an administrator — there is no public self-registration.

## Sign in

1. Open the dashboard in your browser.
2. Enter the email address and password provided by your administrator.
3. After signing in you are asked to pick a **deployment** (for example *Zambia App* or *Malawi App*). Everything you see afterwards belongs to that deployment.

## After signing in

You land on **Home**, a hub of cards linking to every section your role can access. Above the cards, **Latest Updates** surfaces anything waiting for you — for example pending permission requests if you manage users.

Use the deployment switcher in the header at any time to move between country apps. Changing deployment reloads the dashboard in the new context.

<Callout type="tip">
  Lost your password? Open your **Profile** from the avatar menu and use the
  password-reset page — see [Your profile](../account/profile).
</Callout>
