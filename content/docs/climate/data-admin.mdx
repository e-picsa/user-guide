**Climate → Admin** (requires `climate.admin`) is the quality-control desk for climate data. It answers two questions: *is the data-system data healthy?* and *does the field app have the same data?*

## The three tabs

1. **Data System** — every station with its data products (annual rainfall, crop probabilities, annual and monthly temperatures), rainfall issue flags (for example very short records) and per-row download. `Refresh All` re-fetches everything with a progress counter.
2. **App Data** — what is currently bundled with the field app (`Bundled`/`Missing` per station, year ranges, available products). This is what farmers actually see.
3. **Data Comparison (Diff)** — compares the two: totals in sync vs divergent, stations only on one side, added/missing years and value differences. Open a row's preview to see exactly what changed before releasing a new app bundle.

## Typical workflow

1. `Refresh All` (or refresh a single station) in **Data System**.
2. Check rainfall issues and failed product icons.
3. Open **Data Comparison** and resolve divergences until stations show *In Sync*.
4. Regenerate the app bundle so the field app picks up the verified data.

<Callout type="warning">
  Refreshing re-fetches summaries from the climate API and can take a while
  for large station lists — the counter shows progress.
</Callout>
