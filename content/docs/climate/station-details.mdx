## The four tabs

1. **Location** — district, station name and coordinates summary.
2. **Data** — one row per year (season start/end/length, rainfall totals) with pagination and a CSV download named after the station.
3. **Charts** — visual summaries such as Seasonal Rainfall, Start/End/Length of Season, Extreme Rainfall and Min/Max Temperatures. These mirror what farmers see in the field app.
4. **Crop Probabilities** — success probabilities per planting date, with a slider to pick the planting date and Grid/Table views.

## Refreshing a station

**Refresh Data** pulls the latest summaries (annual rainfall, crop probabilities, annual and monthly temperatures) from the climate API. A status dialog shows each product succeeding or failing, so a partial failure is visible rather than silent.

<Callout type="tip">
  Changed data only reaches the field app once the bundled app data is
  regenerated — admins verify this in [Climate data admin](./data-admin).
</Callout>
