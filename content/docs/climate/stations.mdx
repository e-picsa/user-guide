**Climate → Station Data** lists every weather station in the active deployment's country. It is the entry point for all historical climate products.

## What you can do

- **Search and sort** the table to find a station by district or name.
- **Open a station** by clicking its table row or its numbered map marker. This takes you to [Station details](./station-details).
- **Zoom the map** with the +/- controls to inspect station coverage.

<Callout type="info">
  The **Refresh** button in the top-right is only visible to `climate.admin`
  users. It updates station records from the climate API and does not change
  historical data — for that, see [Climate data admin](./data-admin).
</Callout>
