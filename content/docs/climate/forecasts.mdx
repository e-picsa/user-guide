## Browsing forecasts

- Pick a **month** to list only forecasts issued in that month.
- Switch between **Seasonal, Downscaled, Weekly and Daily** tabs.
- Click a row's file icon to open or download the PDF. Missing files are fetched automatically from the climate API.

## Publishing a forecast (admin)

**Add Forecast** opens a form where you upload the PDF, set its label (usually the season or date range), choose the language, and — for downscaled forecasts — pick the location it applies to. The file is stored in your deployment's country storage bucket and recorded in the forecasts table.

<Callout type="info">
  Downscaled forecasts require a location; seasonal, weekly and daily
  forecasts do not. The form guesses the location from the filename when it can.
</Callout>
