**Profile** (from the avatar menu) gathers everything about you: which deployments you belong to, what you may do, your personal details and account actions.

## Sections

1. **Deployments** — all deployments you can access. Selecting one switches context (same as the header picker).
2. **Permissions** — your roles per feature, shown as disabled checkboxes. To change them, ask a `deployments.admin` — see [User Permissions](../managing/user-permissions).
3. **Profile Settings** — full name, country and organisation, saved with **Save Profile**. Unverified emails show a **Verify** action that resends the verification mail. A **password-reset** page lives alongside for changing your password.
4. **Account Management** — request a GDPR data export or account deletion through support.

<Callout type="tip">
  New to a second country? Once an admin adds you to that deployment it
  appears in section 1 — no new account needed.
</Callout>
