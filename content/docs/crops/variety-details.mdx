## Crop details

The base record holds the crop, variety name, maturity period and days-lower/upper range. After creation the crop and variety name are locked (they form the record's ID); other fields remain editable. Admins create new records from **Crop → Variety → Add Variety**.

## Water requirements

Each row maps the variety to one location with the millimetres of water it needs over its growing period. Adding an entry is a two-step dialog: pick the location, then enter the water requirement. These values feed the [probability tables](./probability-tables), so a variety with no water requirements will show a coverage warning there.

<Callout type="tip">
  Bulk-editing many water requirements at once is easier with CSV import —

</Callout>
