**Crop → Variety** is the master list of crop varieties for your deployment, with how many locations each one has water-requirement data for.

## What you can do

- **Search and sort** to find a variety.
- **Open a variety** by clicking its row — see [Variety details](./variety-details).

<Callout type="info">
  **Add Variety** (`crop/variety/add`, `crop.editor`) sits at the top of the
  page above the visible area — scroll up to create a new base record, then add
  its per-location water requirements on the details page.
</Callout>

Each variety records its crop, maturity period (early/medium/late) and days-to-maturity range, which feed the [probability tables](./probability-tables).
