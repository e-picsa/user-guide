## What you can do

- **Search and sort** to find a variety.
- **Open a variety** by clicking its row — see [Variety details](./variety-details).
- **Add a variety** (admin) to create the base record; water requirements per location are added afterwards on the details page.

Each variety records its crop, maturity period (early/medium/late) and days-to-maturity range, which feed the [probability tables](./probability-tables).
