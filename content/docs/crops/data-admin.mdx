**Crop → Admin** (requires `crop.admin`) oversees the per-location water requirements behind every probability table: which districts are covered, which climate station each location is paired with, and whether the field app is in sync.

## Locations coverage

The chips summarise coverage at a glance: districts tracked, locations still missing requirements, paired and unique water-requirement definitions, and how many locations have probability data available. Each table row maps a location to its parent region, its requirement status, and the climate station it is paired with. Selecting a row lets you view or change the linked station, which decides the rainfall data behind that location's probability tables.

## Other tabs

- **Stations** lists stations missing crop information.
- **App Diff** compares the app's bundled probability tables against freshly regenerated ones — reload after syncing or importing to confirm the app is up to date.
- **Duplicates** flags repeated crop and variety definitions.
- **Upload** bulk-imports water requirements from CSV: **Template** downloads a sheet with one row per location, crop and variety; fill in the water-requirement column and **Upload File** (or paste the text) to preview changes. **Rows** shows values to be upserted, **Duplicates** flags repeated lines and **Errors** lists invalid rows; **Import** writes the valid rows (rounded to the nearest 5 mm). **Export App Data** generates the per-location probability tables as JSON and downloads them as a zip — the artefact bundled into the field app release.
