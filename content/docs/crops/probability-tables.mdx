## Reading the table

- The header shows the location, the linked **climate station**, and season-start probabilities per date.
- Each row is a variety with its days to maturity and water requirement; cells like `9/10` mean success in 9 of the last 10 seasons.
- The crop icons above filter the table to one crop.

## Re-linking a station

If a location has no station — or the wrong one — **Change Station** opens a searchable station picker with a map. Updating re-links the location and recalculates the table.

## Sharing

Below the table (scroll down) you will find **Copy To Clipboard** for pasting
into messages or documents, a language picker, and **Export → Word**, which
downloads a `.docx` in the selected language, ready to print or share offline.
Warnings at the top point to missing station data or missing water
requirements, with links to fix them.
